Built for Every Journey

The things that actually matter at speed, and none of the things that don’t.

Says Only What Matters

Turn left, turn right, take your exit. Never “go straight” — roads already do that. On a long highway it just names the road and counts down.

Reroutes Forward

Miss a turn and it finds a way on in about a second — never a U-turn back the way you came.

Stops Along the Way

Before you set off it asks whether you need fuel, food or a restroom, finds them on your route, and slots them in.

Warns You Early

A heads-up half a mile before a tunnel takes your signal, sharp bends called out, and speed cameras named.

Plan the Whole Run

Multi-stop trips with per-leg distance and time, then hand the lot straight to the map.

Honest by Default

No traffic it hasn’t measured, and no invented red on a clear road. When it doesn’t know, it says so.

Find Everything Along Your Route

Tap a category and it searches the road ahead, not just a circle around you.
